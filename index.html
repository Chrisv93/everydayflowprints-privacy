Privacy Policy

EverydayFlowPrints Growth Agent

Last updated: October 11, 2026

EverydayFlowPrints Growth Agent is an internal marketing automation application operated by EverydayFlowPrints in Germany.

Purpose

The application is designed to manage Pinterest marketing activities for our own business, including creating and scheduling Pins, analyzing account performance, and managing advertising campaigns when authorized.

Information Processed

With account authorization, the application may access Pinterest account information, Pins, boards, analytics and advertising data within the permissions granted by the account owner.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to manage and improve our own marketing activities. The application is not intended to access other users' Pinterest accounts.

Storage and Security

The application is being developed for a controlled automation environment. Access restrictions and security measures must be implemented and verified before production use.

Third-Party Services

Pinterest processes information according to its own privacy policy. GitHub Pages is used to host this public privacy notice.

Access Revocation and Deletion

Account authorization can be revoked through the applicable Pinterest account settings. For privacy or deletion requests, contact us using the address below.
